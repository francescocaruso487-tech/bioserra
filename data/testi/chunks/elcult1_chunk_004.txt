W. MilleE (1974) exposed the roots of the mung bean plant (Vicia faba) to electric or magnetic fields at 75 Hz when no indication of an effect on either growth or mitosis was observed. No chromosomal anomalies were seen. The electric field exposure was made in a plastic tank filled with a plant nutrient solution in which there was a current of 0.1 A/m² and an electric field of 1 mV/m. Plants were in a similar tank for which the electrodes were not energized. The magnetic field experiments were conducted at 0.5, 5, and 17 G in plastic cylinders containing the plants in a liquid nutrient medium and wrapped with electrical wire.
